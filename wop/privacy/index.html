WOP privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

WOP is a mobile game published by Phare Studio. This page explains, in plain words, what data the game uses, why, who it is shared with and how to delete it. The rule of thumb: we never ask for your name, email or a password.

Who is responsible

WOP is published under the name Phare Studio by Yanis Devouche, independent developer (France). For any question or request: contact@pharestudio.fun.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (planes, catapults, coins, diamonds, boosters, collection) and your settings are stored on your phone. They are not sent to our servers (only a few figures from your game, such as your record, number of flights, levels and coin and diamond balances, go along with the game statistics, see below). Uninstalling the game erases them.

Game statistics

To know whether players enjoy the game and what to improve, WOP sends pseudonymous usage statistics (they do not say who you are) to PostHog, hosted in the European Union: game opened, flights finished (distance, duration, record), upgrades, daily challenge, ads watched, purchases (the product, never the payment), along with the game version, language, the country set on the phone, phone model and Android version. Diagnostic data is added: game errors (where in the code they happened), smoothness (frames per second, loading time), the phone's memory and graphics chip, and the estimated revenue of each ad shown.

They are tied to a random identifier created at install time, used only for statistics and renewed every 13 months: your nickname, game account and advertising ID are never sent, and the IP address is discarded on receipt. They are used only by us, never for advertising. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in measuring and improving the game. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Game statistics.

Ads

WOP is free and shows ads, mostly videos you choose to watch for a reward. They are served by Google AdMob, which may call other ad networks (such as AppLovin or Unity Ads). These networks use your phone's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show ads, measure them and fight fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your choice on first launch (Google consent form). Without your consent, ads are not personalised. You can change your mind at any time in Settings › Ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. The No ads option removes forced ads.

Ad network policies: Google, AppLovin, Unity.

Who your data is shared with

  • Supabase hosts our server and database in Ireland (European Union).
  • Google (Google Play for purchases, AdMob for ads).
  • The ad networks listed above, depending on your consent choice.
  • PostHog (game statistics), servers in the European Union.

We do not sell your data. Some providers (Google, ad networks) may process data outside the European Union, notably in the United States, under GDPR safeguards (EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

How long

  • Game account, launches, leaderboards and friend codes: until you delete your account.
  • Purchases: 10 years, the legal retention period for accounting records in France. After account deletion they are no longer linked to you.
  • Server technical logs: a few days.
  • Game statistics: 1 year at most.
  • Messages sent through “Send feedback”: as long as needed to reply and handle your feedback.

Delete your account

In the game: Settings › Delete my account. Without the game: email us. Everything is explained on the Delete my WOP account page.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase or port your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Email contact@pharestudio.fun with your nickname; we reply within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Age

WOP is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not designed for younger children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Important changes will be shown in the game.
